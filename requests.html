Student requests

Students ask for help on a question (“samajh nahi aya”) or for a recheck of a mark. You see the requests of your own classes. Your reply appears on the student’s Help & requests page.

Find a question by its ID

Type the ID printed on the question (for example T000012-003). You will see the question, how many students got it right, and who asked about it.
